Day 370: Stink bidding at 1c on Polymarket is one of the most underrated ways to hit a 100x.

Nobody really talks about this strategy, but it works and it works way more often than you’d expect. 

> Find markets with great volume but very thin orderbook
> Set stink bids with close expiry day (7 days for example) 
> Avoid markets that are likely to resolve before your expiry
> Use a Telegram bot to alert you when a bid gets filled 
 
Your goal is simple: wait for someone to accidentally nuke the orderbook.
This can happen when a NO holder market-sells everything or when a YES buyer wipes out the entire side of the book.

Example: 
- edgeX FDV > $1B: $196k volume, but only $1,422 depth on the NO side (instant 13x if hit)
- edgeX FDV > $2B: $60k volume but just $2,559 of liquidity on the NO side (instant 45x if hit)

A 10$ bet at 1c can end up with being a $1000 win.
It only takes one stupid mistake from someone for you to make an easy play with up to 100x ROI.

Trust me this happens way more than you think.
